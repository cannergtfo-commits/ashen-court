# Ashen Court

A dark-fantasy court you can walk. Vaelor and Sereth share one bone rig. Maelveth waits on the path and only fights if you ask.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Keep the `assets` folder beside it.

On Windows, double-click `AshenCourt.cmd`. It opens the game next to the launcher.

Press **Start** (or Enter) once the court is ready. If you press it early, the court opens as soon as the art is keyed. Esc pauses. Title returns to the menu.

## Controls

- PC: WASD or arrows, Space strike, Shift dash, E to open a chest, light a brazier, or ask Maelveth to fight, Tab to swap elves, C for the sheet, R for the bone overlay, M to mute.
- Mobile: the stick moves. Strike, Dash, Light, Swap, and Sheet are on the right.

Click an elf for splash art and current stats against their normal line. Chests hold swords, bows, and arrows.
